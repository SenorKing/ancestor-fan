Regional populations are rounded from broad published estimates (the kind compiled by McEvedy and Jones, the HYDE database and the UN) and are uncertain, often by a quarter or more before 1800. The estimate treats each place in your tree as a random draw from the people alive in your chosen pool that generation, so repeats appear once places outnumber people. Real families are less random than that. Use it for scale, not for genealogy records. A family tree file you load is read inside your browser and is not uploaded anywhere.
